Responsive wireframes

Mobile-ready behavior for the other Phase 1 screens

These are not final visuals. They define how the already planned screens change when the viewport grows: mobile keeps the purchase action close, tablet introduces two-column review states, and desktop opens wider merchandising and operations layouts.

Mobile320-430px
Tablet768-1024px
Desktop1200px+
RuleSame content, different density

R01. Storefront Home

Mobile -> desktop
Full-width launch hero with campaign image, collection story, and primary CTA
New arrivals carousel becomes 2x2 editorial grid
Categories become horizontal pills on mobile, card grid on desktop
Social shopping strip expands to shoppable content row
MobileSingle column, sticky bottom nav, hero CTA above product sections.
TabletHero remains first, product cards move to two or three columns.
DesktopHero can become split editorial/product composition with a persistent top nav.

R02. Catalogue and Product

Discovery scales up
Search, category tabs, sort
Product grid: 2 columns mobile, 3 tablet, 4 desktop
Product media left on desktop, details and options right
Sticky add-to-cart remains on mobile, becomes inline desktop CTA
MobileFilters collapse behind one button; product CTA sticks to bottom.
TabletFilters can be inline chips; product images and details begin to sit side by side.
DesktopUse left filter rail for catalogue and two-column product detail layout.

R03. Cart and Checkout

Conversion protection
Cart items or checkout form in primary column
Delivery note and validation messages sit below relevant fields
Order summary becomes persistent right column on tablet/desktop
Payment and delivery decisions appear as market-aware cards
MobileOne step at a time, sticky CTA, summary close to final action.
TabletForm and summary can sit side by side after cart review.
DesktopPersistent right summary reduces anxiety while customer fills checkout.

R04. Admin Responsiveness

Operational fallback
Desktop sidebar becomes top tab bar on tablet
KPI cards stack 2x2 on tablet, 1 column on mobile
Order table becomes order cards below 840px
Dashboard remains useful on mobile for quick checks, not heavy admin work
Product editor stacks image, fields, stock, and publish controls
MobileUseful for quick order checks and status updates; complex editing is secondary.
TabletGood baseline for fulfilment use during operations.
DesktopPrimary admin viewport for product setup, order review, and settings.

States To Add In Figma

  • Empty cart and no-search-results states.
  • Unavailable size with waitlist capture.
  • Checkout validation and payment-review messaging.
  • Confirmation for Angola, Portugal, and international market variations.
  • Admin empty/loading/error states for orders, products, and settings.

Open Phase 1 Decisions

  • Primary launch market and default currency.
  • Payment methods/provider for Angola and Portugal.
  • Delivery and fulfilment rules.
  • Whether full customer accounts remain Phase 2.
  • Whether admin mobile is for quick checks only or full editing.
